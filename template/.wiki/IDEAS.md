# Ideas

> **Purpose:** Capture exploratory project thinking—rough ideas, possible features, questions, and loose plans—before you are ready to accept a product direction or write a specification. Nothing here is an approved requirement, priority, architectural decision, or delivery commitment.
>
> Keep this lightweight. Add, revise, combine, or discard notes freely. When an idea is accepted, distill it into its durable owner rather than maintaining competing copies here: project purpose and boundaries in [CONCEPT.md](./CONCEPT.md), deferred work in [BACKLOG.md](./BACKLOG.md), committed or imminent work in [TODO.md](./TODO.md), product requirements in [PRD.md](./PRD.md), and feature requirements in a [feature spec](features/README.md).

Use any headings that help. The sections below are optional prompts, not a required checklist.

## Project direction

What might this project become? What possibilities or alternatives are worth exploring?

## Possible products and features

Capture capabilities, audiences, use cases, and other ideas without implying they are in scope.

## Questions and assumptions

What is unknown? Which assumptions might change the direction?

## Loose plans and experiments

Note possible next steps, prototypes, research, or tests. These are suggestions, not an approved roadmap.
